HUGHESY'S LEGISLATION DATABASE - iPHONE OFFLINE EDITION

Includes 26 Acts embedded in index.html. No live automatic updates.

1. Extract this ZIP on a PC.
2. Upload the extracted folder (not just index.html) to https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Open the HTTPS address in Safari on your iPhone.
4. Wait for page to finish loading. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
5. Launch from Home Screen while online once, wait until fully loaded, then test with Airplane Mode.

Notes/favourites are stored locally on the device and do not sync. Do not store sensitive operational or personal information. The uploaded website may be publicly accessible.

Legislation comes from previously supplied PDFs, with mixed dates and unverified text extraction. Always confirm current law at legislation.nt.gov.au.
